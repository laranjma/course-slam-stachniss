Exercise: Particle Filter
(a) Both the particle filter and the Kalman filter are implementation of a Bayes filter. The Kalman filter relies on a parametric form (a Gaussian) to describe the probability distribution of the current state: a mean and a covariance matrix must be computed. The particle filter on the other hand models the distribution by samples (particles) and does not relie on parameters. Each particle represents one possible trajectory of the robot and the its pertinence is calculated based on the observation model in the correction step. The most pertinent particles, from the observatoin point of view, are replicated, the least tend to disappear.
(b) Low variance re-sampling ensures that the particles are replicated proportionally to theirs weights, but with lower variance to avoid diversity is lost too quickly. It means: the particle with the highest weight will not be over replicated in detriment of the particles with moderate weights. Low variance resampling is also time efficient: O(N) complexity.
